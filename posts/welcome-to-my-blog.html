Welcome

Welcome to my corner of the internet 👋

I’m a curious software engineer who likes understanding how things work beneath the surface.

A simple question like “How does this actually work?” can send me down a rabbit hole of research papers, source code, architecture diagrams, experiments, and prototypes. Sometimes for a few hours. Sometimes for a few days.

This blog is where I write down what I discover along the way.

You’ll find me exploring systems, distributed systems, databases, AI, LLMs, inference, GPUs, performance engineering, and the ideas behind the software we use every day.

I’m not here to pretend I have all the answers. Most of these posts start with a question, a problem, or something I don’t fully understand.

I learn it. I break it down. I build things around it. And then I try to explain it simply.

Hopefully, some of those rabbit holes are useful to you too.

Welcome. Let’s figure things out together.
