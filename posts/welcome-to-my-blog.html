Welcome

Welcome to my corner of the internet 👋 I’m a software engineer who is endlessly curious about how things work.I like going down rabbit holes.A simple question like *“How does this actually work?”* can easily turn into hours of reading papers, digging through source code, building a small prototype, or trying to explain the idea in the simplest way possible.That curiosity is what this blog is about.I’ll write about things I’m learning and exploring across **systems, distributed systems, databases, AI, LLMs, inference, GPUs, performance engineering, and software engineering**.Sometimes it’ll be a deep dive into a research paper. Sometimes it’ll be a breakdown of how a system works. Sometimes it’ll just be something interesting I discovered while building or debugging something.I don’t want this to be a collection of polished answers.I want it to capture the process of **figuring things out**.If I learn something interesting, I’ll try to understand it deeply, break it down, and write about it in a way that makes sense to another curious engineer.So, if you enjoy asking *“but how does this actually work?”*, you’ll probably feel at home here.**Welcome. Let’s learn something interesting.**
